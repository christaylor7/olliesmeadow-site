Ollie's Meadow Studio

The publishing tool behind the Ollie's Meadow YouTube channel, made by TA Ventures LLC.

What Ollie's Meadow Studio does

Ollie's Meadow Studio is a small internal application used only by the two people who run TA Ventures LLC. Its single purpose is to publish our own children's videos to our own YouTube channel, Ollie's Meadow.

When a team member signs in with their Google account, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload a finished episode or YouTube Short to the Ollie's Meadow channel;
  • set the video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, "made for kids" setting and AI-content disclosure;
  • schedule when the video goes public;
  • read the channel's name, so it can refuse to upload to any channel other than Ollie's Meadow.

It is not offered to the public, does not have other users, and does not access anyone else's data. It does not read viewers' information, comments, subscribers or watch history.

About Ollie's Meadow

Ollie's Meadow is a calm, low-stimulation video series for children aged 2–5. Ollie and his puppy Kuma explore a quiet meadow, learning colours, counting and everyday discoveries at a gentle pace. Episodes are original stories made by TA Ventures LLC with the help of AI tools, and every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. Our content is wholesome, family-friendly educational material for young children; it never contains sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery of any kind, and we never use Google APIs to create, upload or distribute such material.
