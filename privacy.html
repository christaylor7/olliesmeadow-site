Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Ollie's Meadow website and the internal publishing tool ("TA Ventures Studio Tools") that TA Ventures LLC uses to manage its own YouTube channels.

Website

This website does not collect personal information, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not ask visitors — including children — for any data.

Our publishing tool and Google user data

TA Ventures Studio Tools is used only by TA Ventures LLC staff to upload and manage videos, thumbnails and video details on channels the company owns. It signs in with Google OAuth and requests access to YouTube solely for that purpose.

  • It accesses only the YouTube channel of the person who signs in, and only to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic channel details (such as the channel name, to confirm the right channel).
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the company's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
