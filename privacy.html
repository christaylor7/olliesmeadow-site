Privacy policy — Ollie's Meadow Studio

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information Ollie's Meadow Studio (the "app") accesses, how it is used, stored and protected, and the choices you have. The app is operated by TA Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). It also covers this website.

1. Who the app is for

The app is an internal tool used only by TA Ventures LLC staff to publish videos to the company's own YouTube channel, Ollie's Meadow. It is not offered to the public. Children do not use the app, and it does not collect information from children or from the viewers of our videos.

2. Google user data we access

When a staff member signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • YouTube upload (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) — to upload videos to the signed-in account's YouTube channel.
  • Manage YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube), if granted — to set thumbnails, video details, privacy and scheduled publish times on videos we uploaded, and to read the channel's name.

The data the app reads is limited to: the signed-in channel's ID and name, and the details of the videos we upload (title, description, tags, privacy status, publish time, processing status). The app does not access email, contacts, files, comments, subscribers, viewer data, analytics or watch history.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to upload and manage our own videos on our own channel, and to check that we are uploading to the correct channel. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or to train or improve any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model, and we do not use Google APIs to create or distribute AI-generated intimate or non-consensual imagery.

4. Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only in a private folder on a TA Ventures LLC computer, excluded from source control, and are never published or transmitted anywhere except to Google's servers when calling the YouTube API. Communication with Google uses HTTPS. We keep no separate copy or database of Google user data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share Google user data with anyone. It is not transferred to third parties, except as required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while we need the app to publish videos. To delete them, a user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer act on the account; we also delete the local token file on request or when we stop using the app. You can ask us to delete any data at the contact below and we will do so within 30 days.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Ollie's Meadow Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services: by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information from visitors.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above.
